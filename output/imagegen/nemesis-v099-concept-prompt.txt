Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: art direction concept sheet for the existing NEMESIS PACT browser game, v0.9.9.
Primary request: refine a playable sci-fi bullet hell game where a geometric enemy called THE NOTARY negotiates a binding contract with the player. Build one coherent concept board with three clearly separated screens: large combat view at top right, title view top left, wide negotiation interface along the bottom.
Scene/background: ancient orbital covenant chamber. Matte ink-blue and obsidian architectural panels, engraved concentric brass rings, faint blueprint lines and understated starlight. The Notary is an abstract mechanical hexagonal eye with six armored radial wings and a mint luminous diamond core, not a human character. Small ivory and mint player spacecraft.
Style/medium: art-directed high-end independent game, clean orthographic 3D arena with minimal editorial UI, beautifully restrained contrast. Sharp hard-surface forms, warm brushed brass, pale mint, ivory typography. Approachable readable gameplay, not a film poster.
Composition/framing: combat uses generous empty floor space; enemies at top, player below, well-separated small amber enemy projectiles, magenta laser hazard, a mint sanctuary ring at left. Thin top boss bar, small ship hull display, bottom ability controls and compact signed-contract strip. Negotiation uses two equal columns: identity and voice transcript at left, benefit/price contract and prominent Sign button at right. No desktop page scroll. Title has monumental NEMESIS PACT typography and one mint primary start button with the Notary seal dominating the other side.
Text (verbatim): "NEMESIS PACT", "THE NOTARY", "Name your terms.", "YOUR ADVANTAGE", "YOUR PRICE", "Sign & enter combat", "Start voice".
Lighting/mood: calm, precise, dangerous, ceremonial; geometry visibly lit above a low-contrast arena floor. Restrained glows only at cores and live controls.
Constraints: retain existing top-down combat, two-column contract review, voice and text paths; designs must be plausible to implement with CSS and procedural Canvas/WebGL without a framework rewrite. No external brand logos, no watermark, no photoreal humans, no crowded dashboard, no decorative particles that resemble hostile bullets, no excessive bloom, no unreadable black-on-black gameplay.
