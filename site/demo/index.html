How it plays

Negotiate the rules. Fight your deal.

  1. 01 / SPEAK & SIGN

    Make your enemy an offer.

    Ask for stronger reflections, a safe zone or a different trade-off. Interrupt to revise. GPT-Live-1 handles the conversation; GPT-5.6 Luna proposes the terms. Review the benefit and the price, then Sign to apply them.

  2. 02 / BUILD & FIGHT

    Become the bullet storm.

    Clear waves, choose upgrades and collect relics. Stack orbit satellites, rapid fire, homing shots and echo ships. Dash through danger, parry enemy bullets and unleash a Supernova.

  3. 03 / REVIEW & ADVANCE

    The enemy learns your pace.

    Enable AI analysis to let Luna review wave and sector results. Difficulty shifts in small, bounded steps for the next encounter. Keep your hull, build and signed pact as you push toward the next boss.

Start with First Contact for a short negotiation and boss duel, or choose Full Campaign for six sectors of upgrades and battles. English and Japanese requests are supported. Text negotiation and LOCAL RULES are available when voice or online AI cannot be used.

Recording details & verification

The demo uses a fixed autoplay input policy and original Microsoft Zira synthetic player speech. GPT-Live-1 receives that audio; GPT-5.6 Luna proposes contracts and reviews performance. The captured pressure progresses from 0 to +1 to +2. No invulnerability, changed health, accelerated game clock or forced victory is used.

The uncut run lasts about 10 minutes 27 seconds, including 6 minutes 11 seconds of combat. Thirteen chronological, normal-speed cuts in native DaVinci Resolve preserve the original speech, enemy replies and game audio. The export is 1920 × 1080 at fixed 60 fps: 3,540 frames over 59 seconds. The original capture averaged 58.65 delivered fps; conversion duplicates missing frames without inventing motion.

The film and PC gallery show automated Windows Edge / WebGPU gameplay. The separate earlier iPhone film records human play. Read measurements, costs and validation limits or inspect gallery image provenance.
