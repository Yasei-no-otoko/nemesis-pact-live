01 / SLIDE TO MOVE

Slide on the movement pad in the direction you want to go. Lift your finger to stop. You can also drag on the battlefield. Aiming and firing are automatic.

02 / RETURN FIRE

Tap PARRY as bullets approach. Catch them just after activation for a stronger perfect parry. Keep moving with one finger and parry with the other.

03 / DASH THROUGH

Tap DASH to move briefly unharmed. When stopped, you dash in your last movement direction. Lasers cannot be parried: dash through them.

04 / CLEAR THE SKY

Graze bullets, parry or destroy enemies to charge energy. When full, tap NOVA to erase enemy bullets and lasers. Training gives you unlimited attempts.

05 / KEEP IT. OR BREAK IT.

Tap a card to compare, then confirm below. Pause → Break pact restores 1 hull and clears enemy fire. You lose all pact effects, and enemies attack 25% more often.

01 / MOVE. THE SHIP FIRES FOR YOU.

Use WASD / arrow keys, or slide on the movement pad. Aiming and firing are automatic in First Contact. Your bullet hitbox is the white dot at your center.

02 / RETURN THEIR FIRE

Press E / right-click, or tap PARRY as bullets approach. A well-timed parry sends the barrage back. The cooldown appears on the button.

03 / DASH THROUGH MAGENTA

Press SPACE / SHIFT, or tap DASH while moving. Lasers cannot be parried or stopped by a sanctuary. Dash through them while invulnerable.

04 / SPEND YOUR ENERGY

Parries, grazes and kills charge Nova. When ready, press F or tap NOVA to damage enemies and clear bullets and lasers.

05 / REWRITE THE FIGHT

One amendment per encounter. After 8 seconds, press R or choose Renegotiate in Pause. A parley is offered at 18 seconds. Combat pauses; review and Sign to apply new terms. Hull and boss damage are preserved.

06 / YOUR WORD HAS A PRICE

A sanctuary erases bullets even when you are outside it. All benefits and prices stay active until replaced or broken. Q, or Pause → Break pact, restores 1 hull and clears fire, but removes the deal and makes enemies attack faster.
